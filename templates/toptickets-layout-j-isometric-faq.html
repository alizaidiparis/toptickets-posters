PLAN YOUR VISIT

How long?

About 1 hour inside. Add 1–2 hours for the gardens & Gloriette.

Best time?

The first slot of the morning, before the tour groups arrive.

Getting there?

U4 metro to "Schönbrunn", then a 5-minute walk.

Photos inside?

Not in the state rooms, so save your camera for the gardens.

Gardens?

The main palace park is free to stroll all year.
